WHY HASH HUMANITY

A social network should connect people, not manipulate them.

Modern social platforms can be flooded with bots, fake profiles, duplicate accounts and coordinated account networks. Those accounts can manufacture popularity, amplify narratives and create the appearance of public consensus that may not actually exist.

Hash Humanity approaches that problem at the identity layer. Instead of trying to guess whether an account is a bot after it has already entered the network, HumanKey is designed to establish unique human participation before the account becomes part of the social system.

HUMANKEY

Zero-knowledge proof of personhood for one human, one account.

HumanKey is Hash Humanity's proof-of-personhood protocol for human verified social media. It combines human verification, duplicate detection, privacy-preserving cryptography, zero-knowledge proof technology and passkey authentication to support one human, one account.

The objective is not to publish who you are. The objective is to establish that a unique human is behind the account while keeping the public social identity separate from the person's real-world identity.
