WHY HASH HUMANITY

A social network should connect people, not manufacture reality.

When one operator can control hundreds or thousands of accounts, numbers stop meaning what people think they mean. Likes, replies, trends and apparent consensus can all be manufactured.

Hash Humanity changes the starting condition: establish a unique human first. The goal is not to decide what people should believe. It is to make it harder for fake crowds to impersonate real ones.

HUMANKEY

Prove you're human without making your identity the product.

HumanKey combines adult eligibility and liveness checks with duplicate detection to establish that a unique person is enrolling. After verification, privacy-preserving zero-knowledge proof technology allows that result to be represented without making the biometric check itself your everyday login.
